I'm a Physics MSc student at Imperial College London working at the intersection of particle physics and machine learning. Currenty I'm building contrastive-learning transformer models for the CMS Level-1 trigger as part of my thesis.

I enjoy problems where real data, messy systems, and clever algorithms meet. My background spans detector simulations, data analysis, and ML.

## What I do
- ML for real-time physics: transformer-based representation learning on CMS datasets  
- High-energy physics analysis and simulation  
- Early work in continuous-variable quantum machine learning  
- GPU-accelerated pipelines in JAX/Keras and related tooling
